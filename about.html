Our Story

A place in the Sahyadri that we built from the ground up, with our own hands and vision.

How It Began

Built by Us, From Scratch

Anant Srushti began with a simple idea: to create a place of our own in the Sahyadri. We built the entire property from scratch ourselves, shaping it step by step into the retreat we had imagined.

Set in Majgaon, Velhe, the resort brings together open mountain views, forest surroundings, a seasonal waterfall, and comfortable places to stay. We welcome guests to slow down, spend time outdoors, and enjoy the quiet of the hills.

Our aim is to offer warm hospitality and a relaxed stay for families and friends, with the comforts of a resort close to the natural landscape around us.

Our Setting

In the Heart of the Sahyadri

Located in Majgaon, Velhe Taluka, Anant Srushti is about a two-hour drive from Pune. The old route guide highlights two approaches: via Nanded City, Khadakwasla and Velhe, or via Khed Shivapur, Nasarapur and Velhe.

⛰️

Mountain Surroundings

Take in the open views and changing weather of the Sahyadri, with the historic forts of Torna and Rajgad in the wider region.

💧

Seasonal Waterfall

A waterfall within the property flows during the monsoon season, offering a refreshing way to experience the landscape.

🌿

Time Outdoors

Enjoy nature walks, open-air relaxation, and clear-night stargazing when weather and season allow.
