Tent Stay includes dinner and next-day breakfast. Standard also includes evening high tea and snacks. Premium includes evening high tea and snacks, veg and non-veg BBQ, dinner, and next-day breakfast. Lunch is available as an add-on with all packages; BBQ is an add-on with Tent Stay and Standard.
