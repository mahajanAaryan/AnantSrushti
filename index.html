Our Story

Explore the Forests
of the Sahyadri

Anant Srushti began with a simple idea: to create a place of our own in the Sahyadris. We built the entire property from scratch ourselves, shaping it with care into the retreat we had imagined. Now, near the historic forts of Torna (Prachandgad) and Rajgad, it brings together forest trails, waterfalls, organic farms, and open mountain views.

Enjoy farm-fresh meals, outdoor adventures, a dip in the infinity pool, and a peaceful place to relax with family and friends.

✦ Signature Experience

The Night Sky
Is Your Infinite Canvas

At Anant Srushti, when the sun dips behind the Sahyadri peaks, the real magic begins. Zero light pollution. Crystal clear Deccan skies. Our dedicated stargazing area lets you witness the Milky Way, meteor showers, planets, and constellations — a sky show nature has been performing for billions of years, waiting for you to look up.
