Our Story

Where the Forest Floor
Meets the Milky Way

Tucked into the Sahyadri range near the historical forts of Torna (Prachandgad) and Rajgad, Anant Srushti is built on a simple philosophy: nature is infinite. Look around you — ancient forests, tumbling waterfalls, organic farms humming with life. Then look up — thousands of stars, nebulae, planets, the full canvas of the cosmos.

We've created a space where both dimensions are fully yours to experience. Farm fresh meals, adventure in the wild, a dip in the infinity pool at golden hour — and then, as night falls over the Sahyadris, the real show begins.
