✦ Signature Night Experience

The Sky is Your Infinite Planetarium

Majgaon sits in a rare dark-sky zone — far from city light pollution, nestled in the Sahyadri range where the air is crisp and the nights are crystal clear. On a clear night, you can see thousands of stars, the band of the Milky Way, planets, and sometimes even meteor showers with your naked eye.

Our nature tents are specifically positioned in open lawn areas to give you maximum sky coverage. We keep the property lights minimal during stargazing hours. Whether you're an amateur astronomer, an astrophotographer, or just someone who wants to feel small in the best possible way — this is your place.

💧 Water Experiences

Swim to the Edge of the Mountains

Our Infinity Swimming Pool is one of the most breathtaking features of Anant Srushti. Perched to give the illusion of merging with the Sahyadri valley below, the pool offers panoramic views of the mountain range and the silhouettes of Torna and Rajgad forts. A dedicated Jacuzzi and kids' pool are also part of this spectacular water complex.

During monsoon season (July–September), guests also get access to our private seasonal waterfall — a cascading natural stream right within the resort premises that transforms the landscape into something out of a painting.

🍽️ Farm-to-Table Dining

Rajgad Restaurant

Named after the legendary Rajgad Fort visible from our property, our restaurant serves food grown just metres away from where you eat. Every ingredient is organically sourced from our farm or local Sahyadri producers — guaranteeing flavours that are as pure as the mountain air.

The menu spans authentic Maharashtrian cuisine (misal, poha, upma, local thalis) and Punjabi favourites. BBQ nights, bonfires and outdoor dining under the stars make every meal an experience in itself.
